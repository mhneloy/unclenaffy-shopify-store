# Uncle Naffy Shopify storefront

This Online Store 2.0 theme uses a dedicated layout for the barbering website. The homepage can be previewed immediately after uploading the theme as unpublished. Its navigation falls back to homepage sections until the Shopify pages exist.

## Shopify setup

Create the four page resources with these handles. When the templates are available for assignment in Shopify admin, select the corresponding template. In an unpublished preview, the theme can also resolve the page through its alternate template using the view parameter. Pages with other handles can be selected in **Theme settings → Uncle Naffy storefront**.

| Page | Handle | Template suffix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About Uncle Naffy | `about-uncle-naffy` | `un-about` |
| Book Appointment | `book-appointment` | `un-book` |
| Price List & Gallery | `price-list-gallery` | `un-prices` |
| Location, Hours & Feedback | `location-hours-feedback` | `un-visit` |

In the Theme Editor, select the logo, palette, fonts, links and verified contact details in the storefront settings. The homepage and alternate templates contain editable sections and blocks for media, prices, portraits, opening hours and reviews. Add the approved aerial video through the hero’s Shopify-hosted video picker. The attached aerial and haircut images are illustrative staging assets.

The footer can link to approved policy pages through its policy menu. The `un-privacy`, `un-cookies`, `un-terms` and `un-cancellation` templates include review briefs in the Theme Editor. Add approved wording to the Shopify pages before linking them.

## Booking and launch

Set the approved hosted Acuity scheduling URL in the storefront settings. Enable the inline scheduler if desired and enter the official iframe URL if it differs. Configure services, calendars, hours, reminders, deposits and cancellation in Acuity. Match published prices and durations in the Shopify price list and homepage preview. Verify the hosted link, availability, confirmation, deposits, rescheduling and cancellation with an authorised test booking.

Enable the Shopify feedback form once its privacy notice and receiving inbox are approved. Verify delivery and the storefront consent settings on the enrolled store. Replace the demo media, prices and biography before publication. The advanced staff, loyalty and inventory workflows are outside the configured theme and depend on the approved operational scope.
